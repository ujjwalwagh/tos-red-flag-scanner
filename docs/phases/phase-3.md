# Phase 3: Legal Clause Detection & Rule Engine

## Objectives
- 100% deterministic, local, and offline legal clause rule engine.
- 14 major legal risk categories:
  1. Mandatory Binding Arbitration
  2. Class Action Waivers
  3. Broad User Content IP Licenses
  4. Automatic Subscription Renewal
  5. Indefinite Data Retention
  6. Unilateral Account Termination
  7. Unilateral Agreement Modifications
  8. Extreme Liability Disclaimers
  9. Broad User Indemnification Obligations
  10. Third-Party Data Tracking & Sharing
  11. Restrictive Dispute Jurisdiction / Foreign Forum
  12. Non-Refundable Fee Policies
  13. Strict Waiver of Injunctive Relief
  14. Forced Retroactive Terms Application
- Anti-pattern detection to eliminate false positives.
- Non-definitive, educational plain-English explanations.
- `/api/analyze` endpoint.
