# TOS Red Flag Scanner — User Guide & Workflow Manual

**Comprehensive Guide to Legal Document Analysis, Risk Scoring, and Evidence Q&A**  
**Version:** 2.0 (Phase 7G-E Release)  

---

## 1. Overview & Core Philosophy

TOS Red Flag Scanner allows consumers and legal researchers to understand complex Terms of Service, Paid Subscription Agreements, and Privacy Policies in seconds. It operates on a **zero-retention privacy runtime**: no raw contract text or questions are permanently stored on any server.

## 2. Complete 10-Step User Workflow

```
[ Step 1: Open Website ] ──► [ Step 2: Open Extension ] ──► [ Step 3: Find Documents ]
                                                                       │
[ Step 6: Confirm Document ] ◄── [ Step 5: Extract & Preview ] ◄── [ Step 4: Select Document ]
         │
         ▼
[ Step 7: Risk Dashboard ] ──► [ Step 8: Ask Questions ] ──► [ Step 9: Review Evidence & NLI ]
                                                                       │
                                                       [ Step 10: Multi-Document Switch ]
```

### Step 1: Open Target Website
Navigate in Google Chrome to any website or web application containing legal agreements (e.g. `https://www.spotify.com`, `https://discord.com`, `https://github.com`). Standard public HTTP and HTTPS pages are supported.

> [!NOTE]
> Internal browser pages (`chrome://`, `edge://`, `about:`) and the Chrome Web Store cannot be scanned due to Chrome security sandbox restrictions.

### Step 2: Open the Extension Popup
Click the **TOS Red Flag Scanner** icon in your Chrome toolbar.
- The header displays the active website domain (e.g. `spotify.com`).
- The connection indicator displays a green dot with **"Backend Connected"** when your local backend service is active.

### Step 3: Find Legal Documents
Click the **Find Legal Documents** button.
- The extension inspects the page DOM, examining navigation links, footer elements, and legal link containers.
- Candidate agreements are grouped by category:
  - **Terms of Service:** General terms, user agreements, conditions of use.
  - **Paid Subscription Terms:** Recurring billing, auto-renewal terms, subscription agreements.
  - **Privacy Policies:** Global privacy notices, customer data protection policies.
  - **Other Privacy / Regional Links:** "Do Not Sell or Share My Info", California Privacy Rights, Cookie Policies.
  - **Legal Hubs:** Central legal portals (e.g. `/legal`).

### Step 4: Select the Desired Document
Each candidate displays its title, target URL, detection evidence, and confidence rating (High, Medium, Low).
- Click **Select** next to the document you wish to analyze (e.g., *Privacy Policy*).
- The document is marked as **"Selected ✓"** and the Selection Banner appears with document metadata.

### Step 5: Extract and Preview Text
Click **Extract Selected Document**.
- If the agreement is on the current page, it is extracted immediately.
- If the agreement is hosted on a separate URL or different domain, the extension will request optional host permission if required, open an isolated temporary tab in the background, extract the substantive text, and close the temporary tab automatically.
- The **Preview** tab renders:
  - **Substantive word and character count.**
  - **Heading count.**
  - **Scrollable full text preview.**

### Step 6: Confirm Analysis
To prevent accidental automated scanning of irrelevant pages, click **Confirm Document**.
- The confirmation banner shows confirmed word counts.
- The **Analyze Document** button is unlocked.

### Step 7: Review the Risk Dashboard
Click **Analyze Document**. The backend analyzes the document against 14 risk categories in real time:
- **Overall Risk Meter:** Visual score from 0 to 100 indicating consumer risk severity.
- **Severity Counts:** Total number of High, Medium, and Low risk clauses detected.
- **Readability Index:** Flesch Reading Ease score and equivalent school grade level (e.g., Grade 14 / College level).
- **Interactive Risk Cards:** Expandable clause cards displaying:
  - Clause severity badge (`HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`, `INFO`).
  - Risk category (e.g. `ARBITRATION`, `AUTO_RENEWAL`, `PRIVACY_DATA_SHARING`).
  - Plain-English explanation of what the clause legally means.
  - "Why It Matters" summary of consumer impact.
  - Original verbatim clause quote.

### Step 8: Ask Document-Specific Questions
Scroll down to the **Document Q&A** section:
- Type your question in plain English (e.g., *"Can I cancel my subscription at any time?"* or *"Does this company sell my personal data?"*).
- You can also click any pre-configured question chip for common consumer concerns.
- Click **Ask Question**.

### Step 9: Review Grounded Answers & NLI Verification
The Q&A result displays three layers of verification:
1. **AI Answer:** Plain-English response generated by Gemini 2.5 Pro.
2. **NLI Verification Badge:**
   - `SUPPORTED` (Green): DeBERTa confirms the answer is factually entailed by the original contract text ($\ge 60\%$ entailment).
   - `CONTRADICTED` (Red): DeBERTa detected a direct factual conflict ($\ge 50\%$ contradiction).
   - `UNCERTAIN` (Yellow): Evidence is insufficient or ambiguous.
3. **Supporting Evidence Clauses:**
   - Exact verbatim contract clauses cited by the AI.
   - Genuine similarity scores (e.g., `Similarity: 78%` or `Similarity unavailable` for contextual exception clauses).

### Step 10: Multi-Document Switching
If you switch to another detected candidate (e.g., switching from *Terms of Service* to *Privacy Policy*):
- The dashboard, preview text, and Q&A history instantly switch to the newly selected document.
- Your previous questions and answers for Terms of Service remain safely preserved in session memory and will reappear when you switch back.
- Documents never cross-contaminate each other.

## 3. Oversized Documents & The 100,000-Character Boundary

Certain comprehensive legal terms exceed 100,000 characters.

### Why the Boundary Exists:
To prevent silent truncation—where an AI model quietly drops the last 20 pages of a contract without telling the user—TOS Red Flag Scanner enforces an explicit 100,000-character boundary.

### How the Consent Modal Works:
If an extracted document exceeds 100,000 characters:
1. An alert appears displaying the exact character count (e.g., *142,500 characters*).
2. The extension presents an explicit warning modal:
   > *"This document exceeds the 100,000-character limit. Scanning will analyze the first 100,000 characters, but later clauses will be omitted. Do you want to proceed with partial analysis?"*
3. If you click **Proceed with Partial Analysis**, the document is marked as partial, and a persistent **"Partial Analysis"** warning banner is displayed across the Dashboard and Q&A history so you are always aware of omitted sections.
4. If you click **Cancel**, analysis is aborted and no data is sent to the backend.

## 4. Understanding DeBERTa NLI Classifications

| Status Badge | DeBERTa Label | Meaning for the User | Recommended Action |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **SUPPORTED** | Entailment ($\ge 0.60$) | The answer is directly proven by the cited contract clauses. | Trust the answer; verify the cited clause quote. |
| **CONTRADICTED** | Contradiction ($\ge 0.50$) | The generated answer conflicts with the cited clause facts. | Treat answer as unreliable; read original clause. |
| **UNCERTAIN** | Neutral / Refusal | The contract does not contain enough information to give a definite answer. | Consult customer support or legal counsel. |
| **UNAVAILABLE** | Service Offline | Python NLI service was offline or timed out; answer is unverified. | Ensure Python microservice is running. |

---

## 5. Tips for Best Results

- **Be Specific in Questions:** Instead of asking *"Is it bad?"*, ask *"Can they change subscription fees without prior notice?"* or *"Can I delete my account and personal data?"*.
- **Verify Cited Clauses:** Always expand the **Supporting Evidence** card below the answer to inspect the exact contract paragraph cited.
- **Check Multiple Documents:** If a service has both *Terms of Use* and *Paid Subscription Terms*, cancellation terms are usually found in the *Paid Subscription Terms*, while general liability caps are found in the *Terms of Use*.

## 6. Configuring Backend Settings (Options Page — Phase 8C.3D)

The extension provides a dedicated **Options Page** allowing full control over the backend connection:

### Accessing Settings:
1. In the popup, click the **Settings** button in the header or in the Backend Engine Status card.
2. Alternatively, right-click the extension icon in Chrome and click **Options**.

### Configurable Fields:
1. **Backend Server URL:**
   - **Default:** `http://localhost:3000`
   - **Remote Server:** Supports secure HTTPS endpoints (e.g. `https://api.my-tos-scanner.org`).
   - Plain HTTP is strictly forbidden for remote servers to prevent man-in-the-middle eavesdropping.
2. **API Bearer Token:**
   - **Optional for Localhost:** Leave blank when developing locally with `ENABLE_API_AUTH=false`.
   - **Remote Backend:** Enter the Bearer token configured on the server.
   - Click the 👁 icon to toggle token masking.

### Actions:
- **Save Configuration:** Persists settings to `chrome.storage.local`.
- **Test Connection:** Runs a safe 4-stage connectivity probe:
  - Validates URL structure.
  - Checks host permission for remote HTTPS domains.
  - Queries `/health` for reachability and CORS approval.
  - Tests `/api/analyze/categories` with the Bearer Token to verify authentication without triggering heavy AI pipelines.
- **Reset to Default:** Restores the backend to `http://localhost:3000` and purges any saved Bearer tokens.

### Interpreting Connection Test Results:
- 🟢 **Connection & Authentication Successful:** Server is reachable and the Bearer Token was accepted.
- 🟢 **Backend Connected (Authentication Disabled):** Server is reachable; authentication is not enforced on this instance.
- 🟡 **Authentication Required (HTTP 401):** Server is online, but requires a valid Bearer Token.
- 🔴 **Authentication Failed (HTTP 401):** The entered token was rejected by the server.
- 🔴 **CORS Origin Not Allowed (HTTP 403):** The backend does not recognize this extension's ID. Copy the extension origin from the bottom card and add it to `CHROME_EXTENSION_ORIGIN` in the backend `.env`.
- 🔴 **Connection Failed:** Server is offline or network unreachable.

## 7. Configuring Web Application Settings (Phase 8C.4B)

The web dashboard (`frontend/public/`) includes an integrated **Backend Settings Modal** allowing users and operators to connect to authenticated or remote backend instances:

### Accessing Settings:
1. Click the **⚙ Settings** button in the top navigation bar.
2. The modal displays your active backend configuration, authentication status, and storage notices.

### Configuration Options:
1. **Backend Server URL:**
   - **Default:** `http://localhost:3000` (or empty to use relative same-origin paths).
   - **Remote HTTPS:** Enter `https://api.my-tos-scanner.org`.
   - **Security Restriction:** Remote unencrypted `http://` URLs are strictly rejected. Local loopback addresses (`localhost`, `127.0.0.1`, `[::1]`) are permitted over HTTP for development.
2. **API Bearer Token:**
   - Leave empty if connecting to a standard local server with `ENABLE_API_AUTH=false`.
   - Provide the secret token when connecting to an authentication-enabled server (`ENABLE_API_AUTH=true`).
   - Use the **Show / Hide** button to toggle token masking.

### Actions:
- **Test Connection:** Probes `/health` and `/api/analyze/categories` without consuming AI tokens or performing scans:
  - 🟢 **Success:** Backend reachable and Bearer Token verified.
  - 🟢 **Reachable:** Backend reachable; authentication is disabled.
  - 🟡 **Authentication Required:** Backend requires a Bearer token.
  - 🔴 **Authentication Failed:** Invalid Bearer token.
  - 🔴 **CORS Blocked:** Remote backend blocked cross-origin requests.
  - 🔴 **Network Error:** Backend unreachable or offline.
- **Save Settings:** Stores configuration in `window.localStorage` under key `tos_scanner_config`.
- **Reset to Localhost:** Clears saved settings from `localStorage` and restores the default `http://localhost:3000`.

### Automatic 401 Authentication Alert Banner:
If the web application issues an API call (scan, Q&A, checklist, export) to an authentication-enabled backend and the request fails with HTTP 401:
- A prominent alert banner appears at the top of the interface: *"Authentication required or token expired. Please configure your Bearer Token."*
- Clicking **Configure Token** in the banner opens the Settings Modal immediately.
