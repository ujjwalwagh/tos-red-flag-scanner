About TOS Red Flag Scanner

TOS Red Flag Scanner is a professional document intelligence platform engineered to make complex terms, data sharing provisions, liability waivers, and subscription clauses transparent and negotiable. All processing respects a strict zero-retention memory architecture.

Storage Security & Privacy Notice

Settings and tokens are stored in your browser's local origin storage (localStorage). They are never transmitted to third parties or embedded in network URLs. Note: localStorage is sandboxed per domain, but is not an encrypted hardware vault. For shared workstations, clear credentials after use.
